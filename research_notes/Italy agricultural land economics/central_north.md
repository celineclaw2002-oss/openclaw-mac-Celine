# Central and Northern Italy: agriculture-first land, house optional (2026-10-01)

## Which current listings survive price, productive-scale, water, and operational-viability filters when no house is required?

### Takeaway
Only three current listings merit direct broker diligence under a remote-owner, hired-labor thesis: **Soriano nel Cimino (chestnuts/olives, EUR145k)**, **Ripatransone (predominantly vineyard, EUR400k)**, and **Casalincontrada (3 ha vineyard, EUR95k)**. Soriano is the strongest new land-only lead because the price is low and 7.90 ha are explicitly productive; Ripatransone may have the best absolute farm cash flow but the listing withholds the exact crop-area split; Casalincontrada can approach a 10-year payback only in a strong grape-margin case. No listing is yet evidence-backed enough to call a confirmed 5-10 year agriculture-only investment.

### Cited Findings

#### 1. Soriano nel Cimino, Lazio — first diligence call
- Asking **EUR145,000** for **10.29 ha**, specifically **6.64 ha of chestnut in full production, 1.26 ha olive, and 2.39 ha woodland**; three collabent pre-1967 ruins are included but not needed for the farm thesis. The listing is live on TrovaCasa under broker Med-Imm, ref. 2319A. No well, irrigation system, realized yield, cultivar, DOP status, sales channel, machinery, or annual costs are disclosed. — [TrovaCasa listing](https://www.trovacasa.it/annunci/vt-tc-1767-73261478)
- Productive hectares are therefore **7.90 ha**, and the ask is about **EUR18.4k per disclosed productive hectare** (EUR145k / 7.90 ha). This calculation does not assign productive value to the woodland or ruins. — [TrovaCasa listing](https://www.trovacasa.it/annunci/vt-tc-1767-73261478)

The listing does **not** quantify vineyard, olive, and arable hectares, vine varieties/ages, production, or irrigation source. — [TrovaCasa listing](https://www.trovacasa.it/annunci/ap-tc-29137-73020340)
- The ask is **EUR31.1k per total hectare**, but price per productive vineyard hectare cannot be calculated without inventing the crop split. — [TrovaCasa listing](https://www.trovacasa.it/annunci/ap-tc-29137-73020340)

#### 3. Casalincontrada, Abruzzo — low-ticket vineyard lead

### Inferences
- **Soriano indicative screen:** CREA reports about **5,650 ha** of Lazio fruit chestnuts and roughly **8,000 t average annual production**, implying a regional average near **1.42 t/ha**; a Viterbo market-data mirror reports a late-2025 Castagna di Vallerano DOP quotation of **EUR3.55/kg**. Applying those two external benchmarks mechanically to Soriano's 6.64 chestnut hectares gives about **EUR33.4k chestnut gross revenue**, before quality discounts, grading losses, harvest/maintenance costs, and without assuming DOP eligibility. — [CREA Lazio chestnut presentation](https://valoreincampo.crea.gov.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/01-Stato-dellarte-della-castanicoltura-da-frutto-laziale_CRISTOFORI.pdf); [AgriMercati/ISMEA Viterbo quotation mirror](https://agrimercati.it/ismea-viterbo/castagne-dop-di-vallerano); [Soriano listing](https://www.trovacasa.it/annunci/vt-tc-1767-73261478)
- For a preliminary owner-FCF range, assuming hired field/harvest/marketing costs consume **45-60% of chestnut gross**, the 1.26 ha olives add **EUR2-3k** crop contribution, and remote administration/insurance/reserves cost **EUR3-5k**, Soriano lands around **EUR10-18k/year**. That is an indicative **8-15 year simple payback** before acquisition tax and transaction costs. These cost and olive-contribution inputs are underwriting assumptions, not listing facts; seller accounts could move the result sharply.
- **Ripatransone threshold test:** using the existing research anchor of **EUR3.5-5.0k/ha vineyard contribution**, the property needs about **EUR40k/year owner FCF** for a 10-year payback. If all 12.87 ha were vineyard, the screen would be roughly EUR37-56k after EUR8k remote overhead; if only 8 ha were vineyard, roughly EUR20-32k. Because the listing says only “predominantly vineyard,” no single payback estimate is defensible until the crop map is supplied. — [Ripatransone listing](https://www.trovacasa.it/annunci/ap-tc-29137-73020340); [BMTI annual grape-report archive](https://www.bmti.it/archivio-report-uve-vino/)
- **Casalincontrada threshold test:** at the saved vineyard contribution range of EUR3.5-5.0k/ha and EUR3-5k small-farm remote overhead, indicative owner FCF is **EUR5.5-12k/year**, or about **8-17 years** simple payback. It reaches the desired window only if yields, DOC eligibility/offtake, mechanization, and contractor costs are strong. — [Casalincontrada listing](https://www.case-appartamenti.eu/terreni-agricoli/abruzzo/chieti/vendita/casalincontrada_vigneti_coltivati.php); [BMTI annual grape-report archive](https://www.bmti.it/archivio-report-uve-vino/)
- **Vallerano:** applying the same CREA regional average and EUR3.55/kg reference gives only about **EUR17.1k gross chestnut revenue** before hired costs on 3.4 ha. At EUR125k, it is unlikely to beat Soriano unless its actual yield/grade, DOP realization, and harvest economics are much better. — [CREA Lazio chestnut presentation](https://valoreincampo.crea.gov.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/01-Stato-dellarte-della-castanicoltura-da-frutto-laziale_CRISTOFORI.pdf); [AgriMercati/ISMEA mirror](https://agrimercati.it/ismea-viterbo/castagne-dop-di-vallerano); [Vallerano listing](https://www.trovacasa.it/annunci/vt-tc-24783-72705538)
- Auctions were excluded from the shortlist despite low headline prices because the user asked for operational investment candidates, and the current auction ads do not establish clean possession, crop condition, water rights, or a normal acquisition timeline.

### Gaps
- Portal “live” status proves only that the page resolves on 2026-10-01; most TrovaCasa detail pages do not expose a publication/update date. Brokers must confirm availability and unchanged price.
- Exact annual production, realized farm-gate price, harvest rejection/grade, contractor invoices, subsidies, taxes, insurance, and capex are absent for every survivor.
- Soriano and Vallerano do not disclose water, road conditions through the productive blocks, slope/mechanical-harvest compatibility, phytosanitary status, or DOP enrollment.
- Ripatransone does not disclose hectares by crop; therefore it is impossible to calculate price per productive vineyard hectare without inventing data.

## Do specialty crops offer stronger agriculture-only cash yield than the southern benchmark candidates?

### Takeaway
**Possibly, but only Soriano currently challenges the southern benchmarks on price-to-productive-hectare.** Chestnut can produce a better listing-level payback than central-Italian arable/olive land because Soriano's entry price is unusually low; however, chestnut revenue is highly exposed to grade, annual yield, pest/disease, and labor-intensive harvest. Ripatransone's vineyard could match Ramacca's scale economics, but the missing crop split prevents verification. Most northern hazelnut and central vineyard listings either lack a price, lack water, or are too small.

### Cited Findings
- CREA's March 2026 Lazio presentation states roughly **5,650 ha** in fruit chestnuts, **more than 6,000 farms**, and average annual production around **8,000 tonnes**, demonstrating both a real local operator ecosystem and very small average farm scale. — [CREA Lazio chestnut presentation](https://valoreincampo.crea.gov.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/01-Stato-dellarte-della-castanicoltura-da-frutto-laziale_CRISTOFORI.pdf)
- BMTI reported that 2025 common-chestnut wholesale prices fell as production improved, illustrating meaningful price cyclicality even in a good crop year. — [BMTI 2025 chestnut analysis](https://www.bmti.it/comunicati-stampa-principale/44511/)
- BMTI's grape reports provide annual area, production, and price information by geography and variety; the listings themselves provide none of the realized farm economics needed to distinguish a high-quality contracted vineyard from commodity grapes. — [BMTI grape-report archive](https://www.bmti.it/archivio-report-uve-vino/)
- The 20 ha Viterbo hazelnut asset has the strongest disclosed agronomic infrastructure in this regional sweep, but the missing price and 2022 insertion date disqualify it from the verified under-EUR500k set. — [Case-Appartamenti listing](https://www.case-appartamenti.eu/terreni-agricoli/lazio/viterbo/vendita/viterbo_noccioleto_giovane_irriguo.php)

### Inferences
- Soriano's **EUR18.4k/disclosed productive ha** is cheaper than the prior southern benchmarks on a simple land-and-crop basis and therefore deserves to be compared directly with Ramacca and Chiaramonte. Its preliminary 8-15 year payback overlaps Ramacca's saved 11-16 year range and can beat Chiaramonte's saved 14-20 year range, but only on benchmark—not seller-proven—production.
- The central/northern screen does **not** reveal a general specialty-crop advantage. The apparent edge is listing-specific (Soriano's low ask), not proof that chestnuts, grapes, or hazelnuts intrinsically beat irrigated southern citrus under hired management.
- Mechanizable vineyard and hazelnut operations remain more compatible with a remote owner than fragmented manual olive groves, but water and contracted operators are mandatory diligence items.

### Gaps
- No survivor publishes three-year farm accounts, AGEA/PAC receipts, crop insurance claims, labor contracts, or buyer/offtake contracts.
- The Viterbo chestnut quotation is a market reference, not the price these specific groves realized; quality, DOP registration, size grade, and sales terms are unknown.
- The southern and central assets have not yet been normalized for acquisition taxes, working capital, or one-time equipment purchases.

## What facts remain unknown and must be obtained from the broker?

### Takeaway
The next step is not more portal screening; it is broker diligence on Soriano, Ripatransone, and Casalincontrada. Each should be rejected unless the broker supplies cadastral crop maps, 2023-2025 production/sales, contractor costs, and legally documented water rights where irrigation is claimed.

### Cited Findings
- **Soriano:** the listing supplies planted hectares but no water, cultivar, output, or cost data. — [TrovaCasa listing](https://www.trovacasa.it/annunci/vt-tc-1767-73261478)
- **Ripatransone:** irrigation and mechanical workability are claimed, but exact hectares by crop and water source are omitted. — [TrovaCasa listing](https://www.trovacasa.it/annunci/ap-tc-29137-73020340)
- **Casalincontrada:** the private well and grape types are disclosed, but no yield, DOC registration, vine age, irrigation system, or commercial agreement appears. — [Case-Appartamenti listing](https://www.case-appartamenti.eu/terreni-agricoli/abruzzo/chieti/vendita/casalincontrada_vigneti_coltivati.php)

### Inferences
- Required for **all three**: cadastral parcels and title; exact cultivated hectares; 2023-2025 kilograms/quintals harvested by crop; invoices showing realized price and buyer; contractor/labor invoices; fertilizer, treatments, pruning, fuel/electricity, insurance, tax, and maintenance; PAC/AGEA receipts; included machinery; phytosanitary history; lease/sharecropping/third-party rights; and confirmation that the sale is assets only with no undisclosed farm liabilities.
- **Soriano-specific:** chestnut cultivars and age; DOP enrollment and certification history; grade/size distribution and rejected percentage; gall wasp/blight history; slope and mechanical collector access; road easements; water source; annual pruning/brush clearing; whether the olive hectare is actually harvested.
- **Ripatransone-specific:** a hectare table by vineyard variety/planting year, olives, and arable; DOC/IGT rights; replanting rights; trellis condition; irrigation source, legal concession, flow and annual pumping/consortium cost; mechanized harvest accessibility; cooperative or grape-buyer contracts.
- **Casalincontrada-specific:** precise Montepulciano/Trebbiano split; vine age and missing-vine rate; planting density and trellis; DOC register status and authorized yield; last three harvests; buyer/cooperative settlement statements; well concession, flow test, and whether the well is connected to a full distribution system.

### Gaps
- Broker contact has not yet occurred, so none of the investment cases should be promoted beyond “diligence lead.”
- The asking prices may be negotiable, but no discount should be modeled until a broker confirms seller motivation and current availability.
