# Southern Italy and Islands — Agriculture-First Land, House Optional (2026-10-01)

## Which live listings survive the price, productive-scale, water, and operational-viability filters?

### Takeaway
Removing the house requirement produces one genuinely important conditional lead—Montalto Uffugo at EUR 170k—and two additional land-first diligence leads, Nardò and San Pietro Vernotico. Ramacca remains the best fully disclosed water-and-scale candidate, but its agriculture-only return still looks marginal at the ask. No listing is yet a fully verified 5–10 year winner because none discloses the complete production and cost history needed for remote-owner underwriting.

### Cited Findings
- **1. Montalto Uffugo, Calabria — EUR 170,000; conditional top lead.** The live listing says approximately 7 ha of vineyard plus 2 ha of olive grove, a roughly 500 sqm recently built cellar, a tasting/management building, and winemaking equipment including 14 tanks; it describes the asset as habitable but does not disclose irrigation, yields, vine varieties/ages, sales, or whether production is currently active. This is the strongest price-to-planted-hectare ratio found in the assigned regions, but it is not yet a water-verified survivor. — [RisorseImmobiliari listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/cosenza/vendita-azienda-agricola-montalto-uffugo-5097467.html)
- **2. Ramacca, Sicily — EUR 329,000; best fully disclosed scale/water survivor.** The current mirror listing states 144,669 sqm (about 14.5 ha), a mix of citrus, olives and arable land, a farm described as fully productive and mechanically/logistically equipped, and a large artificial lake said to cover the entire irrigation season. The listing does not quantify hectares by crop, output, included machinery, or a dwelling. — [RisorseImmobiliari listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/catania/vendita-azienda-agricola-ramacca-5043274.html)
- **3. Nardò / Villaggio Boncore, Puglia — EUR 330,000 in the live portal record; strong water/infrastructure lead, not yet an earnings survivor.** The live record gives 13 ha split into 8 ha arable, 4.2 ha olive grove (Cellina di Nardò and Ogliarola Leccese), 0.8 ha mixed fruit, plus 2,500 sqm of newly installed greenhouses with irrigation, a permitted artesian well, and a rainwater basin. The user-facing card says “price on request,” while its embedded current listing record shows EUR 330,000, so the broker must reconfirm price. — [Cercacasa live results](https://cercacasa.it/vendita/regione-puglia/aziende-agricole?pagina=1); [RisorseImmobiliari mirror](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/lecce/vendita-azienda-agricola-nardo-5005972.html)
- **4. San Pietro Vernotico, Puglia — EUR 290,000; high-upside but highly underdisclosed.** The live listing describes an “avviata” farm of 21 ha across arable, olives and almonds, five farm buildings, and an option to purchase another 10 ha. It does not disclose the crop-area split, tree counts, irrigation, production, sales, or building condition. — [RisorseImmobiliari listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/brindisi/vendita-azienda-agricola-san-pietro-vernotico-4891920.html)
- **5. Serdiana, Sardinia — EUR 190,000; water-verified but only 3 ha currently productive.** The live listing states more than 9 ha total: about 3 ha of vineyard with planting rights and 6 ha of free land, served by a functioning artesian well and irrigation network. The unplanted 6 ha cannot be treated as current productive acreage, and planting/replanting capex is not included in the ask. — [RisorseImmobiliari listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/sud-sardegna/vendita-azienda-agricola-serdiana-5120213.html)
- **6. Matera / Contrada Monteigino, Basilicata — EUR 420,000; scale survivor but economics reject.** The live listing states 30 ha, consisting of 23.5 ha organic arable and 6.5 ha pasture. There is no disclosed irrigation, crop history, machinery, or buildings in the listing summary; commodity arable/pasture income density is too low for the mandate at this price. — [RisorseImmobiliari listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/matera/vendita-azienda-agricola-matera-periferia-nord-5100579.html)
- **Other live sub-EUR 500k examples fail productive-scale or disclosure filters:** Floridia is EUR 150k for only 1.75 ha / 1,200 young lemons despite efficient irrigation and shared-well access; the San Cataldo listing is only EUR 63k but gives 3.8 ha merely as “prevalently” olives and almonds with no water or production data; Altamura is EUR 320k for 6 ha olive plus 6 ha arable with no irrigation/production evidence. — [Floridia](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/siracusa/vendita-azienda-agricola-floridia-4824087.html); [San Cataldo](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/caltanissetta/vendita-azienda-agricola-san-cataldo-via-don-boscovia-gabarazona-calvario-4928006.html); [Altamura](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/bari/vendita-azienda-agricola-altamura-5089706.html)

### Inferences
- A strict “all four conditions already verified” interpretation leaves **Ramacca** as the only new/current land-first lead with price, planted-mix evidence, productive status, and explicit irrigation capacity all in one live listing.
- **Montalto Uffugo** should nevertheless be the first broker call because its low acquisition cost and 9 planted hectares create the only credible path to a 5–10 year commodity-crop payback; the missing water and production evidence is a diligence condition, not a small detail.
- **Nardò** has the best disclosed water/legal-infrastructure package after Ramacca, but its economics depend on whether the 2,500 sqm greenhouse is actually operating profitably; passive olive/arable economics alone do not support the ask.
- **San Pietro Vernotico** could rank anywhere from top-three to reject because “21 ha among arable, olive and almond” is not enough to infer productive hectares.

### Gaps
- Montalto: vineyard/olive cultivars and ages, 2023–2026 yields, water source and rights, current operating status, equipment ownership/condition, grape/wine sales channel, labor and cellar costs.
- Ramacca: exact productive hectares by crop, citrus varieties/tree ages, 2023–2026 production and realized farm-gate prices, machinery schedule, lake title/filling rights and pumping cost, contractor cost, PAC/AGEA receipts.
- Nardò: confirmation that EUR 330k is the actual ask, current greenhouse crop and utilization, greenhouse revenue/cost history, olive-tree health after Xylella exposure, well flow and annual energy cost, and whether organic certification is current and transferable.
- San Pietro Vernotico: hectares/tree counts by crop, Xylella status, irrigation source, yields, five-building condition/use, machinery, PAC rights, and whether the farm is actively harvested.
- Serdiana: vineyard cultivar/age/yields, grape buyer/contracts, irrigation cost, replanting rights and capex for the 6 ha free land, labor cost and PAC receipts.

## What hectares, crops, tree counts, water assets, production evidence, and prices are actually verified?

### Takeaway
The live listing evidence is materially stronger for physical assets than for operating performance. Ramacca, Nardò, and Serdiana disclose water; only Ramacca is explicitly described as fully productive, while none of the top five discloses multi-year output, revenue, or hired-labor cost.

### Cited Findings
- **Montalto Uffugo:** EUR 170k; about 7 ha vines + 2 ha olives; roughly 500 sqm cellar; 14 tanks and winemaking equipment; small management/tasting building; no water or production figure disclosed. — [Listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/cosenza/vendita-azienda-agricola-montalto-uffugo-5097467.html)
- **Ramacca:** EUR 329k; 144,669 sqm total; citrus/olive/arable mix; huge artificial lake; listing says fully productive and equipped, but does not disclose the oft-repeated cross-portal “7 ha citrus” split on this live mirror, so that number should remain unverified until broker confirmation. — [Listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/catania/vendita-azienda-agricola-ramacca-5043274.html)
- **Nardò:** hidden record price EUR 330k / public card “price on request”; 8 ha arable, 4.2 ha named olive cultivars, 0.8 ha mixed fruit, 0.25 ha new irrigated greenhouse; artesian well with recently renewed concession and 24 sqm rainwater basin; no tree counts or current output. — [Cercacasa](https://cercacasa.it/vendita/regione-puglia/aziende-agricole?pagina=1)
- **San Pietro Vernotico:** EUR 290k; listing surface 220,000 sqm and prose saying 21 ha of arable/olive/almond; five buildings; “avviata” status; no water, tree counts, crop split, or output. — [Listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/brindisi/vendita-azienda-agricola-san-pietro-vernotico-4891920.html)
- **Serdiana:** EUR 190k; >9 ha total; 3 ha vineyard, 6 ha free land; planting rights; functioning artesian well and irrigation network; no yield or revenue history. — [Listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/sud-sardegna/vendita-azienda-agricola-serdiana-5120213.html)
- **Comparison candidate, Loceri:** the saved current benchmark is EUR 420k for 11.1 ha total, including about 5.8 ha vineyard, drip irrigation, three artesian wells and a 60,000 L tank; the Idealista detail page was access-blocked in this pass, so these facts are carried from the 2026-09-27 verified watch rather than independently refreshed today. — [Idealista URL](https://www.idealista.it/immobile/28879889/)
- **Comparison candidate, Chiaramonte Gulfi:** the saved benchmark is EUR 205k for roughly 6 ha of orange/olive groves described as in full production with drilled wells and an artificial pond; the Immobiliare detail page was access-blocked in this pass, so current availability is not independently verified here. — [Immobiliare URL](https://www.immobiliare.it/annunci/127807360/)
- **Comparison candidate, Paternò / Ponte Barca:** the 2026-09-27 watch recorded EUR 380k for about 8 ha / 2,700 irrigated olives, on-site lake, and a 193 sqm house; this pass did not locate a directly retrievable detail page, so it remains a benchmark rather than a newly verified survivor. — [Immobiliare Paternò land results](https://www.immobiliare.it/vendita-terreni/paterno-ct/)

### Inferences
- “Fully productive,” “active farm,” and “planting rights” are marketing/physical descriptors, not proof of sustainable owner cash flow.
- The operational evidence hierarchy is: multi-year production and costs (none disclosed) > current quantified output (none among the top five) > planted hectares plus water (Ramacca/Nardò/Serdiana) > planted hectares plus processing infrastructure but no water (Montalto) > total area/mixed-crop wording without a crop split (San Pietro Vernotico).

### Gaps
- No top-five listing publishes three years of crop output, agricultural revenue, contractor/labor spend, electricity/water spend, PAC/AGEA payments, insurance, or recurring repairs.
- Portal area fields sometimes represent building area rather than land area; the prose description must control where they conflict.
- Broker confirmation is required before treating embedded/hidden prices, planting rights, equipment, or wells as transferable sale assets.

## Which listings plausibly approach a 5–10 year agriculture-only payback?

### Takeaway
Only Montalto Uffugo plausibly clears the target on conservative commodity-crop contribution assumptions, and even that result is provisional because the listing omits current production and water. Ramacca can approach 10 years only at the top of the prior operating range; Nardò needs meaningful greenhouse profit; San Pietro needs a favorable crop split; Serdiana, Loceri, Chiaramonte and Paternò remain slower-payback assets.

### Cited Findings
- **Montalto Uffugo indicative screen:** applying the saved hired-labor contribution anchors (vineyard EUR 3.5k–5.0k/ha; olives EUR 2.0k–2.5k/ha; support roughly EUR 250/ha) to the listing's 7 ha vines + 2 ha olives, then deducting EUR 6k–8k remote-owner/admin/repair reserve, gives roughly EUR 22.8k–36.3k annual owner FCF. Against EUR 170k, simple payback is about **4.7–7.5 years**. This is an inference, not a claim about actual farm earnings. — [Listing inputs](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/cosenza/vendita-azienda-agricola-montalto-uffugo-5097467.html)
- **Ramacca:** the prior mixed-crop screen estimated EUR 20k–31k owner FCF from the disclosed 14.5 ha crop mix and water asset; at EUR 329k this implies about **10.6–16.5 years** and needs roughly EUR 32.9k/year to hit 10 years. The live listing confirms total area, crop categories, productive status and lake, but not the crop split necessary to tighten that range. — [Live listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/catania/vendita-azienda-agricola-ramacca-5043274.html)
- **Nardò excluding greenhouse economics:** 4.2 ha olives, 8 ha arable, 0.8 ha mixed fruit and area support produce an indicative EUR 8.9k–14.2k owner FCF after EUR 6k–8k remote-owner overhead under the saved contribution anchors. This implies roughly **23–37 years** at EUR 330k; the greenhouse would need to add approximately EUR 19k–24k sustainable annual net contribution for a 10-year total payback. — [Listing inputs](https://cercacasa.it/vendita/regione-puglia/aziende-agricole?pagina=1)
- **San Pietro Vernotico:** at EUR 290k, the farm needs EUR 29k sustainable annual owner FCF for a 10-year payback. Because the listing does not split the 21 ha among olives, almonds and arable, a defensible point estimate is impossible; a predominantly irrigated productive orchard could approach the hurdle, while a mostly arable holding would not. — [Listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/brindisi/vendita-azienda-agricola-san-pietro-vernotico-4891920.html)
- **Serdiana on current plantings only:** 3 ha of vines plus area support, less EUR 5k–7k remote overhead, gives an indicative EUR 5.8k–12.3k owner FCF, or roughly **15.5–33 years** at EUR 190k. Treating the 6 free hectares as productive without planting capex would violate the mandate. — [Listing inputs](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/sud-sardegna/vendita-azienda-agricola-serdiana-5120213.html)
- **Matera reject:** 23.5 ha organic arable plus support and 6.5 ha pasture, less remote overhead, yields only roughly EUR 10k–13k under the saved arable benchmark, implying approximately **32–42 years** at EUR 420k before transaction costs. — [Listing inputs](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/matera/vendita-azienda-agricola-matera-periferia-nord-5100579.html)
- **Prior benchmark comparison:** saved owner-FCF/payback ranges remain about EUR 10.5k–14.5k / 14–20 years for Chiaramonte, EUR 10k–22k / 17–38 years for Paternò, and EUR 15k–27k / 16–28 years for Loceri; no newly verified evidence supports upgrading them. — [Chiaramonte](https://www.immobiliare.it/annunci/127807360/); [Paternò search page](https://www.immobiliare.it/vendita-terreni/paterno-ct/); [Loceri](https://www.idealista.it/immobile/28879889/)

### Inferences
- **Ranking for calls, not purchases:** (1) Montalto Uffugo, (2) Ramacca, (3) San Pietro Vernotico, (4) Nardò, (5) Chiaramonte Gulfi, then Serdiana/Paternò/Loceri.
- Montalto is the only candidate whose screening math creates room for bad years while remaining near the target, but the risk of a stale/inactive winery package is substantial.
- Ramacca remains economically safer than Nardò because the listing says the farm is fully productive and water-secure; Nardò's required greenhouse contribution turns it into a small operating business rather than passive orchard ownership.
- A simple payback is not a purchase valuation: acquisition taxes/notary/legal costs, working capital, crop-cycle volatility and any deferred capex would lengthen all ranges.

### Gaps
- The contribution anchors are screening assumptions, not property financial statements. Actual yields, farm-gate prices and outsourced labor quotes must replace them before any site visit or offer.
- No calculation includes debt service, buyer taxes/fees, renovation, new planting, or terminal resale value.
- At 8%, ten years of flat cash flow has a present-value factor of about 6.71; therefore a property needs annual FCF around 14.9% of purchase price merely to recover the purchase price in discounted operating cash flow over ten years, before transaction costs. None except the high case for Montalto reaches that bar.

## What must be obtained from the broker before a property is investable?

### Takeaway
The next step is not more portal searching; it is a standardized operating-data request to Montalto and Ramacca first, followed by San Pietro Vernotico and Nardò. A candidate should be dropped if the broker cannot provide crop-area maps plus 2023–2026 output, sales and outsourced-labor costs.

### Cited Findings
- Montalto's listing provides planted area and processing equipment but no water, output, revenue or operating-cost data, so all four categories must be requested before trusting the 4.7–7.5 year screen. — [Listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/cosenza/vendita-azienda-agricola-montalto-uffugo-5097467.html)
- Ramacca's listing provides total area, crop categories, productive status and lake but no crop split, lake-right documentation, production history or machinery schedule. — [Listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/catania/vendita-azienda-agricola-ramacca-5043274.html)
- Nardò's listing is unusually specific on hectares and water infrastructure but silent on greenhouse utilization, production, olive condition and earnings. — [Listing](https://cercacasa.it/vendita/regione-puglia/aziende-agricole?pagina=1)
- San Pietro Vernotico's listing gives only total area, broad crop categories and five buildings; it lacks every operating datum needed to select an economic case. — [Listing](https://www.risorseimmobiliari.it/brindisi/vendita-azienda-agricola-san-pietro-vernotico-4891920.html)

### Inferences
- Request, for each candidate: cadastral parcel/crop map; exact productive and irrigated hectares; cultivar, planting year and tree/vine count; 2023–2026 yields; realized farm-gate prices and buyers; annual pruning/harvest/treatment/irrigation/energy/insurance/admin costs; PAC/AGEA receipts; machinery inventory and condition; water permits, flow/capacity and drought history; leases/easements/pre-emption rights; and any environmental/Xylella constraints.
- Require invoices, cooperative delivery slips, tax returns or farm accounts rather than accepting broker summaries.
- Promotion rule: proceed only if normalized owner FCF after a local manager/contractor, routine capex reserve and all recurring costs is at least 10% of all-in acquisition cost in the base case and remains positive under a 20% yield or price shock.

### Gaps
- Broker identities/contact details were visible on the portals but outreach was not authorized or performed in this research pass.
- No title, cadastral, water-right, subsidy-transfer, phytosanitary or pre-emption-right documents were independently reviewed.
