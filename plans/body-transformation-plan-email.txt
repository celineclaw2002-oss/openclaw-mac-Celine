Here is your combined 12–16 week body-transformation plan, built around your current equipment, schedule, activity level, food preferences, and groceries.

GOAL AND STARTING FRAMEWORK

- Objective: reduce waist/lower-back fat while preserving muscle and emphasizing upper chest, side/rear delts, lats, upper back, and arms for a leaner V-tapered build.
- Starting information: age 23, height 180 cm, reported weight approximately 90 kg (to be verified), improvised waist estimate approximately 93 cm (to be replaced with a tape measurement), 10,000–15,000 daily steps, and approximately eight hours of sleep.
- If 90 kg is accurate, a reasonable 12–16 week outcome is roughly 5–8 kg of weight loss while maintaining or improving strength. The visual result and waist trend matter more than a fixed target weight.

WEEKLY TRAINING ROUTINE

General rules:
- Begin lifting days with 5–8 minutes of easy cycling and movement-specific warm-up sets. Do not do 20–25 minutes of cardio before lifting.
- Stop most working sets with approximately two good repetitions in reserve.
- Rest 2–3 minutes after compound exercises and 60–90 seconds after isolation exercises.
- When all working sets reach the top of the prescribed repetition range with clean form, add the smallest available weight at the next session.
- Log every working set. Maintain 10,000–15,000 daily steps without forcing additional walking.

- Incline dumbbell press: 4 sets of 6–10
- Flat machine or dumbbell press: 3 sets of 8–12
- Seated dumbbell shoulder press: 3 sets of 8–12
- Cable fly: 2 sets of 12–15
- Cable or dumbbell lateral raise: 4 sets of 12–20
- Rope pressdown: 3 sets of 10–15
- Overhead cable triceps extension: 2 sets of 12–15

Tuesday — Lower A and core
- Hack squat or leg press: 4 sets of 6–10
- Romanian deadlift: 3 sets of 6–10
- Bulgarian split squat: 3 sets of 8–12 per leg
- Seated or lying leg curl: 3 sets of 10–15
- Calf raise: 4 sets of 10–15

Wednesday — Pull
- Pull-up or lat pulldown: 4 sets of 6–10
- Chest-supported row: 4 sets of 6–10
- One-arm cable row: 3 sets of 10–12 per side
- Straight-arm pulldown: 2 sets of 12–15
- Rear-delt fly: 4 sets of 12–20
- Incline dumbbell curl: 3 sets of 8–12
- Hammer curl: 2 sets of 10–15

Thursday — Recovery conditioning
- Zone 2 cycling: 35–45 minutes at a steady pace at which you can speak in short sentences
- Mobility: 10 minutes for hips, hamstrings, thoracic spine, and shoulders

Friday — Upper-body aesthetic emphasis
- Incline barbell press: 3 sets of 5–8
- Neutral-grip pulldown: 3 sets of 8–12

- Lateral raise: 4 sets of 12–20
- Rear-delt fly: 3 sets of 15–20

Saturday — Lower B and optional intervals
- Front squat, hack squat, or leg press: 3 sets of 8–12
- Hip thrust: 3 sets of 8–12
- Walking lunge: 3 sets of 10 per leg
- Leg curl: 3 sets of 10–15
- Calf raise: 3 sets of 12–20
- Hanging knee raise: 3 sets of 10–15
- Optional bike intervals: 6 rounds of 1 minute hard and 2 minutes easy

Sunday — Full rest
- Normal light walking is fine. Do not add compensatory cardio.

Training phases:
- Weeks 1–2: learn the movements, establish working weights, and keep approximately two repetitions in reserve.
- Weeks 3–7: progress repetitions or load whenever the progression rule is met.
- Week 8: deload only if fatigue is high, joints ache, or performance is declining; halve the working sets and keep the loads comfortable.
- Weeks 9–16: resume progression and continue using the nutrition-adjustment rules below.

CALORIE AND MACRO TARGETS

Use these starting targets for the first 14 accurately tracked days:
- Calories: 2,600 per day, with a practical tolerance of plus or minus 100 calories for label differences
- Protein: at least 180 g per day
- Fat: 70–85 g per day
- Carbohydrate: use the remaining calories, usually approximately 280–310 g
- Fiber: 30–40 g per day
- Water: approximately 3 liters daily, with more when sweating heavily

No breakfast is required. Suggested timing:
- Lunch: 12:00–2:00 p.m.
- Protein snack when listed or needed: 4:00–6:00 p.m.
- Dinner: 8:00–10:00 p.m.

Water, plain tea, and black coffee are fine before lunch. Meat quantities below are raw weights. Rice and pasta quantities are dry weights.

NUTRITION EXECUTION RULES

- Create recipes in your calorie-tracking app before cooking and divide each recipe by the actual number of portions.
- Weigh oil, avocado, cheese, rice, pasta, and fatty meat rather than estimating them visually.
- If minced meat is fattier than 90% lean, reduce added oil first and starch second.
- Season freely with cumin, pul biber, isot, oregano, sumac, mint, paprika, curry, garlic, lemon or lime, vinegar, and fresh herbs.
- Omit poblano peppers if you do not enjoy them.
- Rice, potatoes, and pasta may be exchanged using equal-calorie portions.
- Chicken breast and shrimp are relatively lean. Salmon, chicken thighs, minced meat, and short rib are not calorie-equivalent and must be logged separately.
- Whey is a convenient protein tool, not a mandatory meal. Use 30–40 g when lunch and dinner alone will not reach 180 g protein.
- Include at least two fruit servings and approximately 500 g of vegetables daily.
- Do not eat back calories reported by a watch or cardio machine.

ALCOHOL

For the fastest visible result, cap alcohol at 0–4 standard drinks weekly. Log each drink, do not starve beforehand to save calories, and alternate alcoholic drinks with water. Six to eight drinks can consume much of the weekly deficit and impair training recovery.

TRACKING AND CALORIE ADJUSTMENT

- Obtain a reliable scale and flexible measuring tape.
- Record morning weight on Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday after using the bathroom and before eating or drinking. Compare weekly averages.
- Measure the waist at the navel every Sunday morning.
- Take relaxed front, side, and back photos every four weeks under the same conditions.
- Hold 2,600 calories constant for the first 14 accurately tracked days.
- Target loss: 0.3–0.8% of body weight per week, approximately 0.27–0.72 kg if the starting weight is 90 kg.
- If average weight loss remains below 0.3% per week for two consecutive weeks with at least 90% adherence, reduce the daily target to 2,450 calories.
- If average loss exceeds 0.8% per week, strength declines materially, or recovery deteriorates, increase the daily target to 2,750 calories.
- Never adjust calories because of one isolated weigh-in.

OPTIONAL SUPPLEMENTS

- Creatine monohydrate: 5 g daily
- Optimum Nutrition whey: use as needed to meet the protein target
- No fat burner is required.

This is a starting framework, not medical care. Avoid crash dieting, punitive cardio, or all-or-nothing food rules. If exercise causes pain, dizziness, faintness, chest symptoms, or other concerning symptoms, stop and seek qualified medical advice.
