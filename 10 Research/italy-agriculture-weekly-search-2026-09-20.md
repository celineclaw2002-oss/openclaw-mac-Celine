# Italy Agriculture-First Property Weekly Search — 2026-09-20

## Mandate and method

Screened current public listings at a maximum asking price of `EUR 500,000`, requiring an existing house or apparently habitable building, agriculture as the primary thesis, no obvious major capex, and plausible operation through hired local labor.

Coverage included targeted searches across `Immobiliare.it`, `Idealista`, `Casa.it`, `TrovaCasa` / `Trovit`, `Gate-away`, `Green-Acres`, and specialist / local agency pages. The August shortlist was re-verified rather than repeated automatically.

The saved underwriting anchors were retained:

- citrus net contribution: `EUR 2.5k-2.8k/ha`
- olives: `EUR 2.0k-2.5k/ha`
- vineyards: `EUR 3.5k-5.0k/ha`
- arable: `EUR 0.45k-0.5k/ha`
- support: roughly `EUR 250/ha`
- then subtract remote-owner overhead / administration / repairs / reserve capex

## Bottom line

No listing is a clean, evidence-backed `5-10 year` agriculture-only breakeven at its current asking price.

One new lead, `Ramacca`, is good enough to displace the old benchmark as the first diligence call because its productive scale is much larger. It reaches the edge of a 10-year simple payback only in an optimistic operating case and still has a material building-status ambiguity.

#### Judgment

- **potentially better than Chiaramonte Gulfi**, because productive scale and disclosed water are stronger
- not yet demonstrably better because the crop split, production history, included equipment, and true habitable-house status are not disclosed cleanly
- first seller / agent diligence call this week

## Market verdict

`Slightly improved`, solely because Ramacca is a credible new diligence candidate and may be better than Chiaramonte on productive scale. The broader market remains thin, and no verified listing clears a disciplined `5-10 year` agriculture-only breakeven.

## Immediate diligence questions for Ramacca

1. Confirm the dwelling's cadastral category, legal habitability, exact square meters, and condition.
2. Provide exact hectares by citrus variety, olives, and arable crop.
3. Provide annual production, realized farm-gate prices, and contractor / labor costs for `2023-2025` plus `2026 YTD`.
4. Confirm lake ownership, filling rights, annual water / electricity cost, irrigation system, and drought history.
5. List machinery and equipment included in the sale, with age and condition.
6. Provide PAC / AGEA receipts, taxes, insurance, and routine repair spending.
