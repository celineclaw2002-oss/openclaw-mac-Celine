# Italy Agriculture-First Property Weekly Search — 2026-09-27

## Mandate and method

Screened current public listings at a maximum asking price of `EUR 500,000`, requiring an existing habitable house, agriculture as the primary thesis, no obvious major capex, and plausible operation through hired local labor, with a `5-10 year` agriculture-only simple payback treated as the mandate.

Coverage this week: `Immobiliare.it`, `Idealista`, `Casa.it`, `TrovaCasa` / `Trovit`, `Subito`, `Cercacasa`, `Green-Acres`, `Gate-away`, `Properstar`, `Engel & Völkers`, `Toscana Houses`, and local agency pages across Sicily, Sardinia, Calabria, Basilicata, Puglia, Lazio, Umbria, Marche, Abruzzo, Piedmont, and Emilia-Romagna. Portal detail pages on `Immobiliare`, `Casa.it`, and `Idealista` blocked direct retrieval; those facts were corroborated through search snippets and mirror portals.

Saved underwriting anchors retained:

- citrus net contribution: `EUR 2.5k-2.8k/ha`
- olives: `EUR 2.0k-2.5k/ha`, with an irrigated-intensive upside case of about `EUR 3.0k-3.5k/ha`
- vineyards: `EUR 3.5k-5.0k/ha`
- arable: `EUR 0.45k-0.5k/ha`
- support: roughly `EUR 250/ha`
- then subtract remote-owner overhead, administration, routine repairs, and reserve capex (`EUR 6k-8k/year` for a mid-size farm)

## Bottom line

No listing clears a disciplined `5-10 year` agriculture-only payback at its current asking price.

One new diligence lead surfaced: `Paternò, Ponte Barca` (Catania), an `8 ha` irrigated olive farm with a `193 sqm` house at `EUR 380k`. It is bigger than Chiaramonte and better watered, but olive-only economics and an ambiguous house condition keep it at a `17+ year` payback on disclosed facts. `Ramacca` remains the first call.
