# Italy Agriculture-First Property Weekly Search — 2026-10-04

## Mandate and method

House optional (rule of `2026-10-01`). Maximum ask `EUR 500,000`. Agriculture-only returns for a remote owner using hired local labor. Target `5-10 year` simple payback on an all-in basis.

Underwriting standard (per `reports/Italy agricultural land economics.md`):

- acquisition basis `1.21x` ask (`15%` registration tax for a non-IAP buyer plus `6%` transaction reserve)
- crop operating margin (MO) from CREA-RICA or ISMEA cost monitoring, not listing gross margins
- remote-owner overhead `EUR 6k-12k/year`, renewal reserve `EUR 250-500` per productive hectare
- CAP/PAC support `zero` unless AGEA receipts and transferability are documented
- ten-year payback requires recurring FCF of `10%` of all-in cost; a candidate that reaches ten years only in the optimistic case is a diligence lead, not a fit

Coverage: `Immobiliare.it`, `Idealista`, `Casa.it`, `TrovaCasa` / `Trovit`, `Subito`, `Caasa`, `Cercacasa`, `Properstar`, `Green-Acres`, `Engel & Völkers`, `Toscana Houses`, local agencies; land-only categories searched explicitly (`terreno agricolo`, `noccioleto`, `agrumeto`, `limoneto`, `pistacchieto`, `mandorleto`, `vigneto`, `frutteto`, `castagneto`, `uva da tavola`, `kiwi`). Immobiliare / Idealista / Casa.it detail pages block direct retrieval; facts were corroborated through search snippets and mirror portals.

## New crop benchmarks gathered this week

- **Oranges, Sicily east (ISMEA 2025 cost monitoring, Tarocco, 10-20 ha farms):** cost `EUR 7,018/ha`, yield `26.5 t/ha`, cost `EUR 0.26/kg` vs average price `EUR 0.44/kg`; implied MO about `EUR 4.8k/ha` for a well-run mature grove. CREA-RICA 2022-24 orange MO `EUR 1.5k-2.0k/ha` remains the conservative anchor. Screening range used: `EUR 1.5k-4.8k/ha` mature irrigated.
- **Hazelnut, Lazio (Tonda Gentile Romana):** ISMEA origin quote early Sept 2026 `EUR 4.00/kg` in shell; Ronciglione cooperative grid `EUR 6.0-8.5` per yield point (about `EUR 2.8-3.9/kg`). Normal Tuscia yields `20-30 q/ha` irrigated; 2025 Lazio output fell about `70%`; 2026 Tuscia up about `50%` but still below potential. CREA-RICA 2021 hazelnut: yield `21 q/ha`, price `EUR 204/q`, gross margin `EUR 3,914/ha` before labor and machinery. Screening range used: yield `12-22 q/ha`, price `EUR 3.0-4.0/kg`, hired-labor costs `EUR 2.5k-3.5k/ha`, so MO `EUR 0.5k-5.3k/ha`, base about `EUR 2k/ha`.
- **Pistachio, Sicily (CREA-RICA, Terra e Vita Feb 2026):** PLT `EUR 5,434/ha`, gross margin `EUR 4,671/ha` averaged across the biennial cycle; hand harvest on lava terrain keeps MO well below gross margin. Screening MO `EUR 2k-5k/ha`.
- **Lemons, Sicily:** no RICA MO extracted; screened at `EUR 7k-8k/ha` costs against `EUR 0.5-0.9/kg` farm gate.
- **Table grapes, Puglia:** farm-gate `EUR 0.30-0.40/kg` reported Sept 2026 with high packing/labor costs; excluded as a passive-owner crop.

## Bottom line

**Zero listings prove a `5-10 year` agriculture-only payback.** The opportunity set did improve physically: land-only hazelnut parcels in the Tuscia (Viterbo) are the first specialty, irrigated, mechanisable crop found at `EUR 22k-44k` per planted hectare, and a Paternò orange grove is the first Sicilian citrus listing with a stated output figure. Every survivor is a diligence lead whose base case sits near zero FCF and whose optimistic case only approaches ten years.

## Market verdict

`Improved`. The land-only rule has now surfaced a crop category, Tuscia hazelnut, with entry prices of `EUR 22k-44k` per planted hectare, installed irrigation on two parcels, mechanised harvest, and a cooperative sales channel. That is the profile the Oct 1 report said to look for. The investment conclusion is unchanged: no candidate has documented yields, prices, or costs, and the base case on every lead is near zero FCF after remote-owner overhead. Nothing clears `5-10 years` on evidence.
