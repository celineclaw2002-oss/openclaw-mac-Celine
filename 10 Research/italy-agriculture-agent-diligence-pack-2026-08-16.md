# Italy Agriculture Agent Diligence Pack

## Purpose

This pack is designed for the top shortlisted Italian farm listings so that broker / seller conversations focus on the exact facts that determine whether the property can work as a strict agriculture-first investment.

The point is to move quickly from:

- attractive listing copy

to:

- real operating facts
- real annual cash generation
- real maintenance burden
- real investment decision

## Top-priority targets

1. `Chiaramonte Gulfi, Sicily`
2. `Spello, Umbria`
3. `Acquaviva Picena, Marche`

Reserve follow-up only if needed:

4. `Patti, Sicily`

## Universal diligence objective

For each property, determine whether it can realistically satisfy all of the following:

- agriculture is the main economic engine
- the house does not require meaningful hidden capex
- the farm can be run through hired local labor / contractors
- annual free cash flow can plausibly justify the purchase within roughly `5-10 years`

## What must be requested from every agent

## 1. Production history

Request:

- annual production for the last `3-5` years by crop
- yield units:
  - grapes: `quintali`, `tons`, or kilograms
  - olives: kilograms of olives and liters / kilograms of oil if processed
  - citrus: kilograms / tons sold
- whether production has been stable, rising, or falling

Why it matters:

- listing phrases like “in production” or “full production” are not enough
- actual yield history is the cleanest test of whether the agricultural thesis is real

## 2. Exact cultivated area by crop

Request:

- exact hectares or square meters by crop
- which portion is actually cultivated today versus only cadastrally classified that way
- age of each planting block
- cultivar / varietal details

Why it matters:

- listing summaries often overstate productive area
- age and varietal mix materially change expected economics

## 3. Revenue and sales channel

Request:

- last `3` years of agricultural revenue by crop
- whether product is sold:
  - wholesale
  - to a cooperative
  - to local buyers
  - direct-to-consumer
  - bottled / branded
- whether there are standing customer relationships or contracts

Why it matters:

- good yield with weak commercialization still fails the investment test

## 4. Annual operating cost structure

Request:

- annual labor / contractor cost
- pruning cost
- harvest cost
- irrigation / water / energy cost
- fertilization and treatment cost
- machinery maintenance cost
- insurance
- property tax and other recurring ownership costs

Why it matters:

- remotely managed farmland can look cheap until labor and small maintenance drag are included

## 5. Water and irrigation

Request:

- exact irrigation source:
  - well
  - pond / basin
  - consortium
  - municipal
- whether water rights are documented
- annual irrigation cost
- whether irrigation serves all productive land or only part
- condition and age of irrigation infrastructure

Why it matters:

- water is one of the biggest hidden differentiators in southern properties

## 6. Equipment and infrastructure

Request:

- complete equipment list
- ownership status
- age / condition / service history
- whether equipment is included in the sale
- condition of:
  - cellar
  - warehouse
  - sheds
  - photovoltaic system
  - fencing
  - internal roads

Why it matters:

- “equipment included” has real value only if the equipment is usable and suited to the property

## 7. House condition

Request:

- whether the house is fully habitable today
- year of last meaningful renovation
- roof condition
- moisture / structural issues
- electrical / plumbing / heating condition
- known deferred maintenance
- estimated near-term capex required in the next `24` months

Why it matters:

- the whole mandate depends on avoiding a disguised capex project

## 8. Legal and cadastral status

Request:

- cadastral maps
- land registry extracts
- zoning / planning status
- whether all structures are compliant
- any easements, rights of way, or disputed access
- whether any land is leased out or subject to agricultural rights

Why it matters:

- agricultural properties often have quiet legal friction that kills the economics later

## 9. Subsidies and incentives

Request:

- CAP / PAC or regional subsidy receipts for the last `3` years
- whether subsidies transfer with the property
- any organic certification or agricultural enrollment status

Why it matters:

- small farms can look mediocre before subsidy and acceptable after it

## Deal-killer thresholds

If any of the following are true, the property should likely be downgraded or dropped:

1. no meaningful production history exists despite the listing implying active production
2. the house needs material near-term renovation
3. irrigation is weak, partial, undocumented, or expensive relative to crop economics
4. productive area is materially smaller than implied in the listing
5. annual contractor / labor costs consume most of the gross farm contribution
6. commercialization depends on an informal seller relationship that will disappear after sale
7. equipment is obsolete, missing, or not actually included

## Property-specific diligence focus

## 1. Chiaramonte Gulfi, Sicily

### Main underwriting uncertainty

This is the best screen candidate, but we do not yet know the exact split between orange grove and olive grove, nor the actual production economics.

### Questions that matter most

1. How many hectares are planted with citrus and how many with olives?
2. What are the last `3-5` years of production and revenue separately for citrus and olives?
3. Are the groves currently maintained and harvested every year?
4. How old are the trees and what cultivars are present?
5. Are the wells permitted and sufficient for normal irrigation?
6. What is the annual cost of irrigation, labor, pruning, and harvest?
7. Is the `110 sqm` house fully usable today without near-term works?

### Decision rule

Stay interested only if:

- production history is real and reasonably stable
- irrigation is robust and documented
- the house does not hide capex
- annual net economics look materially better than the current conservative base case

## 2. Spello, Umbria

### Main underwriting uncertainty

This looks like a real operating farm business, but the asking price only works if the business is genuinely functioning and transferable.

### Questions that matter most

1. What are the exact hectares of vineyard, olive grove, and seminative land?
2. What has been produced and sold in each of the last `3-5` years?
3. Are there established grape / oil / crop buyers?
4. What annual operating costs sit behind the current business?
5. What is included in the equipment package and in what condition?
6. What is the real contribution of the cellar, warehouse, and photovoltaic system?
7. Does the house or farm infrastructure need near-term capital spending?

### Decision rule

Stay interested only if:

- production history is strong
- infrastructure is truly transferable and usable
- farm economics support the price better than the listing alone suggests

## 3. Acquaviva Picena, Marche

### Main underwriting uncertainty

The agricultural setup is attractive, but the price is high for an agriculture-only thesis. This property needs exceptional vineyard economics to justify deeper pursuit.

### Questions that matter most

1. What are the last `3-5` years of grape yields by varietal block?
2. How are the grapes sold today and at what approximate realized pricing?
3. Is the organic positioning certified, current, and economically valuable?
4. What is the full annual cost base for vineyard management and harvest?
5. Exactly which tractor / atomizer / other equipment is included and what is its condition?
6. Is the farmhouse truly in good condition or only cosmetically acceptable?
7. Are there any known replanting needs or vineyard health issues?

### Decision rule

Stay interested only if:

- vineyard yields and commercialization are clearly strong
- organic positioning is real and monetizable
- the included equipment and farmhouse materially reduce future capex

## Reserve: Patti, Sicily

This should only move up if the top three disappoint.

Key questions:

- exact productive split between olives, vineyards, and fruit trees
- actual annual production
- slope / mechanization practicality
- irrigation and contractor economics
- whether the house is truly sound

## Broker message template

Use this as the base outreach text.

### English version

Hello, I am evaluating this property as an agriculture-first investment rather than a lifestyle purchase. Before deciding whether to move forward, could you please send the following:

- production history for the last 3-5 years by crop
- exact cultivated area by crop
- annual agricultural revenue and main sales channels
- annual operating costs for labor, harvest, irrigation, treatments, and maintenance
- details on water sources and irrigation rights
- full list of included equipment and its condition
- confirmation of the current condition of the house and any near-term works needed
- cadastral / land registry documents and any relevant planning or compliance notes
- any subsidy receipts or organic certification details

If easier, I am happy with a simple summary plus whatever documents are already available.

### Italian version

Buongiorno, sto valutando questo immobile come investimento agricolo, non come acquisto lifestyle. Prima di decidere se procedere, potreste gentilmente inviarmi le seguenti informazioni:

- storico della produzione degli ultimi 3-5 anni per coltura
- superficie effettivamente coltivata per ciascuna coltura
- ricavi agricoli annui e principali canali di vendita
- costi annui di manodopera, raccolta, irrigazione, trattamenti e manutenzione
- dettagli sulle fonti d'acqua e sugli eventuali diritti di irrigazione
- elenco completo delle attrezzature incluse e relativo stato
- conferma dello stato attuale dell'abitazione e di eventuali lavori necessari nel breve termine
- documentazione catastale / visure e ogni nota urbanistica o di conformita rilevante
- eventuali contributi PAC / certificazioni biologiche

Se e piu semplice, va benissimo anche un riepilogo sintetico con i documenti gia disponibili.
