# Italy Agriculture Weekly Search — 2026-10-01

## Mandate change

- House is optional from this run onward.
- Asking price remains capped at `EUR 500,000`.
- Economics must work from agriculture alone for a remote owner using hired local labor.
- Target remains a `5-10 year` simple payback, with no credit for appreciation, owner labor, hospitality, branded direct sales, redevelopment, or unverified CAP/PAC support.

## Corrected underwriting framework

- Use crop operating margins from CREA-RICA where relevant; these allocate labor and machinery and are more appropriate than gross-margin-style listing screens for an absentee owner.
- Screen acquisition basis at `1.21x` asking price: `15%` agricultural-land registration tax for a buyer not qualifying as a direct farmer/IAP plus a `6%` transaction/diligence reserve.
- Deduct `EUR 6k-12k` of annual remote-owner administration/oversight and `EUR 250-500` per productive hectare for renewal reserves.
- Base-case CAP/PAC support is zero unless AGEA receipts, eligibility, and transferability are verified.
- A ten-year payback requires recurring annual free cash flow equal to `10%` of all-in acquisition cost.

## Verdict

The house-optional rule improves the entry-price and planted-hectare opportunity set, but **no current listing defensibly meets the 5-10 year agriculture-only payback target**. The result is a broker-diligence queue, not a purchase shortlist. Montalto and Soriano should receive standardized evidence requests first.

## Required broker evidence

- cadastral schedule and parcel-level crop map
- exact productive and irrigated hectares
- cultivar, planting year, density, and missing-tree/vine rate
- 2022-2025 harvest quantities and buyer/cooperative settlement invoices
- contractor, payroll, machinery, pruning, treatment, fertilizer, harvest, irrigation-energy, insurance, and maintenance costs
- water concessions, authorized volume, pump history, irrigation map, and drought/interruption record
- AGEA/PAC statements and transfer terms
- easements, leases, agrarian pre-emption rights, and phytosanitary history
