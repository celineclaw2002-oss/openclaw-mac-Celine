# Morgan Stanley Personal Trading and Outside Research Compliance Pack

## Purpose

Prepare the exact questions Can should get answered before Morgan Stanley onboarding or early in the process so there is no ambiguity around:

- personal trading
- freight derivatives research
- prediction-market activity
- automation and bots
- outside intellectual property and side-project boundaries

This is not legal advice. It is a question pack designed to reduce blind spots.

## Why this matters

The research ideas are promising, but the real constraint may not be technical.

Potential blockers include:

- event-contract restrictions
- derivatives restrictions
- pre-clearance rules
- account-disclosure requirements
- rules on automated trading
- outside-business-activity concerns
- IP ownership or firm-resource constraints

It is much better to ask narrowly and clearly now than to discover later that a good project is unusable.

## Recommended framing

Do not ask one vague question like:

- "Can I trade prediction markets and work on freight ideas?"

Instead, break the issue into separate buckets:

1. what can be personally traded
2. what can be researched but not traded
3. what can be automated
4. what counts as an outside activity
5. what requires disclosure or approval

## Questions to get answered

## A. Personal trading scope

1. Are personal trades in **prediction markets or event contracts** permitted at all?
2. If permitted, are they treated differently from ordinary listed securities or exchange-traded derivatives?
3. Are **freight derivatives or FFAs** categorically prohibited, restricted, or subject to special pre-clearance?
4. Are there any restrictions on trading **crypto-linked products**, including contracts tied to BTC or ETH thresholds?
5. Are there restricted product lists, restricted venues, or prohibited account types that would cover Kalshi, Polymarket, freight brokers, or offshore venues?

## B. Venue and account disclosure

1. Would personal accounts on **Kalshi** need to be disclosed, approved, or linked to compliance surveillance?
2. Would any crypto wallet or account used for **Polymarket** activity need to be disclosed?
3. Are there rules against maintaining accounts on venues that the firm cannot surveil directly?
4. Are there any restrictions on foreign or crypto-native venues even if no trades occur after start date?

## C. Automation and bots

1. If a product is otherwise permitted, is **automated trading** allowed in personal accounts?
2. Are there restrictions on using scripts, agents, APIs, or bots for personal trading?
3. Does strategy automation require additional approval even if the underlying asset class is permitted?
4. Are there restrictions on running automated monitoring or paper-trading systems even if they do not place live orders?

## D. Research versus trading

1. Am I permitted to conduct **independent research** on prediction markets if I do not trade them?
2. Am I permitted to conduct **independent research** on freight derivatives if I do not trade them?
3. Does building models, datasets, or backtests on these markets count as a reportable outside activity?
4. Is there a difference between general educational research and a monetizable trading project?

## E. Outside activity and side-project boundaries

1. Would maintaining a personal research project on prediction markets or freight derivatives count as an **outside business activity**?
2. If the project is unpaid and exploratory, does that change the answer?
3. If the project later becomes a managed account, fund, paid subscription, or signal product, what approvals would be required?
4. Are there restrictions on collaborating with outside contacts on a private market-research project?

## F. Intellectual property and work-product boundaries

1. If I do research in these areas entirely on my own time and equipment, does Morgan Stanley claim any ownership rights?
2. Are there policies that limit work on trading strategies adjacent to the desk's domain even if done privately?
3. Are there restrictions on reusing general techniques, code, or research ideas developed before joining?
4. Are there policies against maintaining a personal research codebase in parallel with the job?

## G. Information-barrier and MNPI risk

1. If I work in fixed income sales strats, are there heightened restrictions on trading event contracts or macro-sensitive instruments because of possible exposure to sensitive information?
2. Would freight or macro event research raise any special concerns because it could overlap with market-moving information flows?
3. If I learn information through work that could affect these external projects, what are the expectations for shutting down or walling off related research?

## H. Timing and grandfathering

1. If an account or project exists before start date, does that change any disclosure or approval requirements?
2. Are there transition rules between signing, pre-start, and actual employment start?
3. Is there a recommended time to ask these questions: now, after signing, at onboarding, or after joining?

## Best way to ask

### Recommended sequence

1. Ask broad policy questions first.
2. Ask venue-specific questions second.
3. Ask project-specific hypotheticals third.

This avoids sounding like there is already hidden activity to defend.

### Recommended tone

Be straightforward and compliance-friendly:

- you want to stay well inside policy
- you are asking before acting
- you are happy to avoid anything prohibited

## Suggested concise email version

I wanted to clarify the firm's policies around personal trading and independent market research so I can make sure I stay fully within compliance rules. In particular, I would appreciate guidance on whether personal activity in prediction markets or freight derivatives is permitted, whether those products or venues require disclosure or pre-clearance, whether automated personal trading is restricted even where trading is otherwise allowed, and whether maintaining an unpaid independent research project on these markets would count as an outside activity or raise any IP or compliance concerns. I’m asking proactively so I can avoid doing anything that would be inconsistent with firm policy.

## What Can likely needs to provide later

- exact venues of interest
- whether live trading is contemplated or only research
- whether any automation would place live orders or only monitor and paper trade
- whether any outside collaborators or commercial intent exist
