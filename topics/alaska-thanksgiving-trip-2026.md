## Seasonal facts verified 2026-10-06

- Fairbanks late Nov: ~5.5 hrs daylight (sunrise ~9:45, sunset ~3:15), typical -5 to -20F, aurora season Aug 21 to Apr 21, aurora visible ~4 of 5 clear nights. Plan 3+ nights there.
- Alaska Railroad Aurora Winter Train runs Sat northbound (ANC 8:30am to FAI 8pm) and Sun southbound (FAI 8:30am) through May 9, 2027. Midweek runs only Feb-Mar.
- Matanuska Glacier: guided only Oct-May; winter tours from Anchorage ~8 hrs, $330-406 incl. gate fee ($55) and lunch; ice caves most stable Nov-Mar. Self-drive guided trek $150 + gate fee at Mile 102.
- Chena Hot Springs: dog sledding $75/20 min (snow-dependent, typically from 2nd week Nov), Aurora Viewing Tour on Charlie Dome $125, Ice Museum $20, 60 mi from Fairbanks.
- Shooting: Rabbit Creek Shooting Park (ADF&G, Anchorage; rifle, pistol, trap/skeet, range masters). Alaska Ammo Fairbanks (340 Merhar Ave, 907-374-8008): indoor 25 yd, rentals incl. pistols/rifles/shotguns/machine guns, $20/hr lane, group bay rentals, 21+ for handguns. Fairbanks Trap Club is members-only.
- Hunting: most big-game seasons closed late Nov; non-residents need a guide for brown bear/sheep/goat. Recommend range/clay shooting instead.
- Thanksgiving Day: several attractions closed or shortened hours.
