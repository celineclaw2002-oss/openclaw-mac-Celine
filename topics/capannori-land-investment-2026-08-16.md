# Capannori Land Investment - 2026-08-16

## Summary

Reviewed the appraisal report for the rural property complex at Via Di Tofori 97 in Capannori / Camigliano and turned it into an investment analysis focused on land profitability, redevelopment economics, and key constraints.

Built a second-pass agriculture-only DCF to test whether the property works at a `EUR 350k` entry price using only vineyard, olive, arable, subsidy, and agricultural exit-value assumptions.

## Main conclusion

This is **not** compelling as a pure land-yield investment.

The stronger potential investment case is:

- restoration plus hospitality / agriturismo / event positioning

The weakest investment case is:

- passive farming or a simple buy-fix-flip

On the new farm-only DCF:

- `EUR 350k` is too high for a disciplined base-case agricultural underwriting
- it only starts to make sense in a relatively optimistic operating case
- rough positive-NPV purchase range is materially lower, around:
  - `~EUR 189k` on a 5-year base case
  - `~EUR 129k` on a 10-year base case

## Key reasons

- the land mix is attractive but not enough by itself to make the economics exciting
- renovation capex is very heavy relative to the acquisition price
- landscape restrictions and technical/legal verification are major gating items
- the vineyard/olive/woodland mix helps scenic and hospitality value more than passive annual yield
